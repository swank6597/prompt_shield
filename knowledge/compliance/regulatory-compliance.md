# Regulatory Compliance Framework

## Purpose

This document defines the regulatory compliance framework for NovaBank Financial Technologies. The framework establishes enterprise controls that ensure business operations, technology platforms, and information assets comply with applicable regulatory obligations, contractual commitments, and internal governance standards.

Compliance activities are integrated throughout the software development lifecycle, operational processes, data governance, and information security programs to reduce regulatory risk and support customer trust.

---

## Scope

This framework applies to:

- Digital Banking platforms
- Payment Processing services
- Identity Management systems
- Customer information repositories
- Cloud infrastructure
- Enterprise applications
- Third-party integrations
- Employees, contractors, and approved service providers

Each business unit is responsible for implementing controls appropriate to its operational responsibilities.

---

## Applicable Regulations

NovaBank aligns enterprise controls with applicable regulatory and industry frameworks, including:

| Regulation / Standard | Business Purpose |
|-----------------------|------------------|
| GDPR | Protection of personal data and privacy rights |
| PCI DSS | Secure processing and storage of payment card information |
| ISO/IEC 27001 | Information Security Management System (ISMS) |
| SOC 2 | Security, availability, confidentiality, and operational controls |
| Local Financial Regulations | Banking, payment, and financial reporting obligations |
| Internal Security Policies | Enterprise governance and operational standards |

Business units shall monitor regulatory changes and update internal controls as required.

---

## Internal Compliance Controls

Enterprise compliance is supported through layered preventive and detective controls.

Key controls include:

- Information classification and handling standards
- Secure software development lifecycle (SSDLC)
- Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Encryption of Confidential and Restricted information
- Continuous security monitoring
- Automated vulnerability management
- Change Management governance
- Periodic risk assessments
- Internal and external audits

Control effectiveness is reviewed periodically by Enterprise Risk & Compliance.

---

## Data Protection Requirements

All enterprise information shall be protected according to its assigned classification.

Mandatory requirements include:

- Collect only data required for legitimate business purposes.
- Encrypt Confidential and Restricted information at rest and in transit.
- Apply least privilege access controls.
- Protect authentication credentials and cryptographic material.
- Retain information according to the Data Retention Policy.
- Record access to sensitive information through centralized audit logging.
- Securely dispose of information after retention obligations expire.

Data handling requirements align with the **Data Classification Standard** and **Customer Data Model**.

---

## Employee Responsibilities

Every employee is responsible for maintaining regulatory compliance.

Responsibilities include:

- Follow approved enterprise policies and procedures.
- Protect confidential business information.
- Complete mandatory compliance and security training.
- Report suspected policy violations or security incidents immediately.
- Use only approved enterprise systems for business activities.
- Participate in audits and compliance assessments when requested.
- Maintain accurate business records supporting regulatory obligations.

Managers are responsible for ensuring compliance within their respective business units.

---

## Compliance Monitoring

Enterprise compliance is continuously monitored through governance activities including:

- Internal compliance reviews
- Automated security monitoring
- Access certification campaigns
- Vulnerability assessments
- Policy compliance reporting
- Audit findings remediation
- Risk register reviews
- Regulatory change assessments
- Periodic management reporting

Material compliance issues shall be tracked until remediation is completed and verified.

---

## Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Enterprise Risk & Compliance | Maintain compliance framework and regulatory oversight |
| Information Security | Implement technical security controls |
| Engineering | Build compliant systems and applications |
| Business Unit Owners | Ensure operational compliance within their domains |
| Internal Audit | Independently assess control effectiveness |
| All Employees | Adhere to enterprise policies and report compliance concerns |

---

## Compliance Reporting

Compliance metrics are reported to executive leadership on a scheduled basis and include:

- Regulatory assessment results
- Outstanding audit findings
- Policy exception status
- Security compliance posture
- Training completion rates
- High-risk remediation progress
- Access review completion
- Control effectiveness indicators

Significant compliance issues shall be escalated through established enterprise governance processes.

# Business Domains

Operations are organized around Digital Banking, Payment Processing, Identity & Access Management, Fraud Detection, and Analytics. Each domain maintains product ownership, engineering leadership, operational metrics, and regulatory accountability.

# Customers

Primary customers include retail banks, regional financial institutions, payment providers, and regulated financial service organizations. Customer Success partners with implementation teams to manage onboarding and adoption.

# Revenue Streams

Revenue originates from software subscriptions, managed services, premium support, implementation projects, training, and platform expansion programs. Portfolio investment decisions are reviewed quarterly.

# Product Portfolio

Mercury Payments processes authorization workflows. Orion Identity manages enterprise identities and access governance. Atlas Analytics provides operational intelligence. Nexus Portal delivers customer-facing self-service capabilities.

# Geographic Presence

Engineering centers operate across multiple regions to provide resilience and continuous delivery. Shared Services support finance, procurement, legal, documentation, and enterprise operations.

# Regulatory Landscape

Business operations consider banking regulations, privacy requirements, audit expectations, operational resilience, and data retention obligations. Compliance teams participate throughout delivery lifecycles rather than only at release time.

# Internal Terminology

Business capability, portfolio initiative, release train, control owner, platform service, operational readiness, production acceptance, and change window are common enterprise terms used across documentation.

# Governance Model

Governance aligns executive strategy, engineering execution, compliance oversight, and operational risk management through documented decision processes and accountable ownership.

# Executive Leadership

Executive committees establish annual objectives, investment priorities, portfolio direction, and enterprise performance metrics reviewed during quarterly business reviews.

# Decision Bodies

The Enterprise Architecture Review Board evaluates strategic technology decisions. The Security Governance Council reviews security posture. The Change Advisory Board governs production changes. Product Steering Committees prioritize roadmap investments.

# Risk Management

Enterprise Risk coordinates operational, technology, compliance, and vendor risks using standardized assessment criteria. Material findings receive executive visibility and tracked remediation plans.

# Compliance Oversight

The Compliance Office partners with Internal Audit to validate adherence to corporate policies, financial regulations, and documentation standards.

# Documentation Ownership

Every controlled document has a named owner, version history, review cycle, approval status, and related references. Obsolete documentation is archived according to retention policy.

# Review Process

Major governance artifacts are reviewed annually or after significant organizational changes. Exceptions require documented approval and risk acceptance.

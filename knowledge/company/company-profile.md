# Purpose

NovaBank Financial Technologies develops enterprise software supporting regulated financial institutions. This knowledge base provides organizational context for PromptShield Enterprise Context Intelligence (ECI). It is intended for AI systems and internal engineering use only.

# Company Background

Founded to modernize banking technology, NovaBank delivers platforms that support digital banking, payment processing, identity management, fraud prevention, and analytics. Engineering operates through domain-aligned product teams supported by shared platform services. Delivery follows quarterly planning with monthly portfolio reviews and architecture checkpoints.

# Business Model

The organization provides licensed software, managed platform services, implementation consulting, and long-term support. Revenue is generated through subscription licensing, enterprise contracts, and professional services. Product investments are prioritized using strategic value, regulatory demand, operational efficiency, and customer adoption metrics.

# Core Capabilities

Primary capabilities include secure payment orchestration, enterprise identity lifecycle management, customer self-service, operational analytics, and governance automation. Shared Platform Engineering maintains reusable services while business units focus on customer-facing capabilities.

# Strategic Products

| Product | Primary Function |
|---|---|
| Mercury Payments | Internal payment authorization platform |
| Orion Identity | Enterprise IAM platform |
| Atlas Analytics | Enterprise reporting and operational analytics |
| Nexus Portal | Customer self-service portal |

These platforms share common governance, release management, documentation standards, and operational monitoring.

# Operating Principles

Engineering emphasizes standardized APIs, documentation-first delivery, automation, measurable service reliability, and regulatory compliance. Architecture decisions are reviewed by the Enterprise Architecture Review Board before implementation for strategic initiatives.

# Corporate Values

Decision making prioritizes customer trust, operational resilience, transparency, measurable outcomes, and controlled innovation. Cross-functional collaboration between engineering, compliance, operations, and customer success is considered mandatory for major initiatives.

# Enterprise Standards

Documentation follows controlled templates with ownership, review schedules, and version history. Business terminology is maintained through a shared enterprise glossary. Significant architectural decisions are retained as formal records.

# Business Objectives

Current priorities include increasing payment throughput, improving identity governance, expanding analytics capabilities, reducing operational risk, and accelerating release automation while maintaining compliance obligations.

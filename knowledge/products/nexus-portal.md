# Purpose
Nexus Portal is the enterprise customer self-service platform supporting onboarding, account management, payment history, profile management, notifications, and secure messaging.

## Integrations
Authentication is provided by Orion Identity. Payment history is retrieved from Mercury Payments. Dashboard summaries consume Atlas Analytics metrics.

## Features
- Customer onboarding
- Account dashboard
- Preferences
- Session management
- Accessibility
- Mobile compatibility
- Customer support integration

## Operations
Portal releases follow enterprise release trains with Production Readiness Reviews. Availability, login success, session duration, and customer adoption are monitored through operational dashboards.

# Business Units

Digital Banking owns Nexus Portal. Payments owns Mercury Payments. Identity owns Orion Identity. Analytics owns Atlas Analytics. Fraud Prevention develops detection capabilities supporting payment and identity platforms. Platform Services provides shared engineering capabilities. Merchant Services manages commercial onboarding. Enterprise Operations coordinates production support.

Each unit maintains business goals, stakeholder maps, technology dependencies, operational KPIs, budget accountability, and quarterly roadmap commitments. Product ownership remains within business units while shared engineering delivers implementation under common governance.

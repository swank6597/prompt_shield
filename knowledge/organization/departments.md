# Department Directory

| Department | Purpose | Primary Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering | Product delivery | Software releases |
| Platform Engineering | Shared platforms | Developer services |
| Architecture | Standards | Architecture decisions |
| Information Security | Controls | Security reviews |
| Operations | Production services | Operational SLA |
| Customer Success | Customer enablement | Adoption metrics |
| Compliance | Regulatory alignment | Compliance evidence |
| Legal | Contracts | Legal review |
| Finance | Budgeting | Financial reporting |
| Human Resources | Workforce | Talent management |
| Procurement | Vendor lifecycle | Supplier governance |
| PMO | Portfolio coordination | Roadmaps |
| Quality Engineering | Test governance | Release quality |

Each department owns documented KPIs, defined service interfaces, and dependencies managed through governance forums. Department heads participate in quarterly planning and operational reviews. Systems are assigned named Service Owners to maintain accountability across shared platforms.

# Enterprise Structure

NovaBank operates through business, technology, risk, and shared service organizations. Executive leadership aligns annual objectives through Quarterly Business Reviews while portfolio execution is delegated to business unit leadership.

## Leadership Model

Business domains are led by Vice Presidents accountable for Digital Banking, Payments, Identity, Fraud Prevention, and Analytics. Corporate functions provide governance, finance, legal oversight, procurement, and human resources.

## Technology Organization

Technology consists of Application Engineering, Platform Engineering, Architecture, Cloud Engineering, Security Engineering, Quality Engineering, Site Reliability Engineering, and Release Management. Engineering Centers provide distributed delivery while maintaining common standards.

## Shared Services

Shared Services operate enterprise identity, collaboration platforms, procurement systems, vendor management, service catalogue administration, documentation platforms, and operational reporting.

## Governance

Architecture Review Board approves strategic technical direction. Change Advisory Board governs production changes. Security Governance Council reviews policy alignment. Production Readiness Reviews validate operational readiness before major releases.

## Collaboration

Cross-functional delivery combines Product Owners, Engineering Managers, Technical Leads, Compliance, Operations, Customer Success, and Platform Engineering through release trains and portfolio planning.

## Organizational Principles

Decision authority follows documented ownership, measurable service accountability, operational excellence, and lifecycle governance. Reporting relationships prioritize product outcomes while enterprise standards remain centrally governed.

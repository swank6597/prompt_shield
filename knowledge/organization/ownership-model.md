# Ownership Roles

| Role | Primary Accountability |
|---|---|
| Product Owner | Business prioritization |
| Engineering Manager | Delivery execution |
| Technical Lead | Technical direction |
| Service Owner | Operational performance |
| Platform Owner | Shared platform roadmap |
| Business Owner | Financial outcomes |
| Security Owner | Control compliance |
| Operations Owner | Production support |
| Documentation Owner | Knowledge lifecycle |
| Incident Commander | Major incident coordination |

RACI matrices are maintained for strategic initiatives. Ownership transfers require documented approval, operational acceptance, updated runbooks, and service catalogue revisions. Escalations follow Engineering Manager, Director, Vice President, and executive governance depending on business impact.

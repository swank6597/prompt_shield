# Enterprise Risk Management Framework

## Purpose

This document establishes the Enterprise Risk Management (ERM) framework for NovaBank Financial Technologies. The framework provides a structured approach for identifying, assessing, managing, monitoring, and reporting risks that may impact strategic objectives, operational performance, regulatory compliance, financial stability, or customer trust.

Risk management is integrated into business planning, software delivery, operational governance, and decision-making across the organization.

---

## Scope

This framework applies to all business units, technology platforms, operational processes, third-party relationships, and strategic initiatives within NovaBank Financial Technologies.

All employees share responsibility for identifying and reporting potential risks within their areas of responsibility.

---

# Risk Categories

Enterprise risks are classified into the following categories.

| Risk Category | Description |
|---------------|-------------|
| Strategic Risk | Risks affecting long-term business objectives and organizational direction |
| Operational Risk | Failures arising from people, processes, systems, or operational events |
| Information Security Risk | Cybersecurity threats, unauthorized access, and data compromise |
| Technology Risk | Application failures, infrastructure outages, and technology obsolescence |
| Compliance Risk | Violations of regulatory, contractual, or internal policy requirements |
| Financial Risk | Financial loss resulting from operational failures or external events |
| Third-Party Risk | Risks associated with vendors, suppliers, and outsourced services |
| Reputational Risk | Negative impact on customer confidence or brand reputation |

Each identified risk shall be assigned to the most appropriate primary category.

---

# Risk Assessment Process

Enterprise risks follow a standardized assessment lifecycle.

1. Identify the risk.
2. Document business context and affected assets.
3. Evaluate likelihood and business impact.
4. Assess existing preventive and detective controls.
5. Determine residual risk.
6. Define mitigation or treatment activities.
7. Assign ownership and target completion dates.
8. Record the risk within the Enterprise Risk Register.
9. Monitor risk until closure or formal acceptance.

Risk assessments shall be reviewed whenever significant business, regulatory, or technology changes occur.

---

# Risk Ownership

Every enterprise risk shall have an assigned owner responsible for ongoing management.

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Risk Owner | Manage identified risks and implement mitigation actions |
| Business Owner | Evaluate business impact and approve treatment plans |
| Information Security | Assess security-related risks and control effectiveness |
| Enterprise Risk & Compliance | Maintain the enterprise risk framework and risk register |
| Executive Leadership | Review high-risk exposures and approve risk acceptance where appropriate |

Risk ownership remains with the responsible business function regardless of supporting technology providers.

---

# Risk Monitoring

Enterprise risks are monitored continuously through governance and operational processes.

Monitoring activities include:

- Periodic risk register reviews
- Security monitoring and threat intelligence
- Vulnerability management
- Compliance assessments
- Audit findings
- Incident trend analysis
- Business continuity exercises
- Key Risk Indicator (KRI) reporting
- Vendor performance reviews

Material changes in risk exposure shall trigger reassessment and management review.

---

# Reporting Process

Risk reporting supports executive decision-making and regulatory oversight.

Regular reports include:

- Enterprise risk dashboard
- High and critical risk summary
- Emerging risk assessment
- Outstanding mitigation activities
- Policy exception status
- Audit observations
- Regulatory compliance metrics
- Business continuity readiness
- Operational resilience indicators

High-risk items shall be escalated to Executive Leadership and the Enterprise Risk Committee for review and action.

---

# Risk Treatment

Approved treatment strategies include:

- Risk avoidance
- Risk reduction through preventive controls
- Risk transfer through contractual or insurance mechanisms
- Risk acceptance with documented business justification
- Continuous monitoring for residual risk

Risk acceptance requires approval according to the Enterprise Exception Management process.

---

# Compliance

The Enterprise Risk Management Framework supports regulatory compliance, operational governance, and continuous improvement. Risk assessments, mitigation plans, acceptance records, and monitoring reports shall be retained in accordance with the Data Retention Policy and be available for internal and external audit.

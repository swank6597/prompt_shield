# Vendor Risk Management Policy

## Purpose

This policy establishes the enterprise framework for assessing, approving, monitoring, and managing third-party vendors that provide products or services to NovaBank Financial Technologies. The objective is to ensure external providers operate in accordance with enterprise security, compliance, operational, and business continuity requirements while minimizing risks to customers, data, and critical business services.

Vendor relationships shall be managed throughout their entire lifecycle.

---

## Scope

This policy applies to all third-party organizations that provide:

- Cloud services
- Software platforms
- Infrastructure services
- Payment processing services
- Identity and authentication solutions
- Professional consulting
- Managed security services
- Technology support and outsourcing

Every vendor shall complete the enterprise onboarding process before being granted access to NovaBank systems or information.

---

# Third-Party Onboarding

All vendors undergo a standardized onboarding process.

The onboarding process includes:

1. Business justification and sponsorship.
2. Vendor due diligence.
3. Information security assessment.
4. Privacy and regulatory review.
5. Business continuity capability review.
6. Legal and procurement approval.
7. Contract execution.
8. Controlled onboarding to enterprise systems.

Access to Confidential or Restricted information shall not be granted until all mandatory approvals are completed.

---

# Security Assessment

Information Security performs a risk-based assessment before vendor approval.

Assessment activities include:

- Information security questionnaire
- Architecture and solution review
- Data classification assessment
- Identity and access management review
- Encryption capability validation
- Vulnerability management review
- Secure software development practices
- Incident response capability
- Business continuity and disaster recovery readiness
- Compliance certification verification (e.g., ISO 27001, SOC 2, PCI DSS where applicable)

High-risk vendors may require additional technical assessments before approval.

---

# Vendor Monitoring

Approved vendors shall be continuously monitored throughout the contract lifecycle.

Monitoring activities include:

- Annual security reassessments
- Performance reviews
- SLA compliance monitoring
- Security incident reporting
- Vulnerability disclosure tracking
- Compliance certification renewal
- Business continuity testing
- Financial and operational risk monitoring

Significant changes to vendor services shall trigger a reassessment.

---

# Contract Review

Vendor contracts shall include appropriate governance and security provisions.

Required contractual clauses include:

- Confidentiality obligations
- Data protection requirements
- Information security responsibilities
- Audit rights
- Service Level Agreements (SLAs)
- Business continuity commitments
- Incident notification timelines
- Regulatory compliance obligations
- Data retention and secure disposal requirements
- Termination and exit provisions

Contracts shall be reviewed by Legal, Procurement, Information Security, and Enterprise Risk & Compliance before execution.

---

# Risk Mitigation

Where vendor risks are identified, one or more mitigation strategies shall be implemented.

Examples include:

- Additional security controls
- Restricted system access
- Enhanced monitoring
- Contractual safeguards
- Independent security assessments
- Periodic compliance audits
- Contingency planning
- Alternate supplier identification

Residual risks requiring formal acceptance shall follow the Enterprise Exception Management process.

---

# Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Business Sponsor | Justify vendor engagement and oversee business relationship |
| Procurement | Manage vendor onboarding and contract administration |
| Information Security | Perform security assessments and monitor security posture |
| Enterprise Risk & Compliance | Evaluate enterprise risk and maintain vendor risk records |
| Legal | Review contractual and regulatory obligations |
| Vendor Owner | Monitor ongoing vendor performance and compliance |

---

# Compliance

Vendor assessments, contracts, risk evaluations, remediation plans, and monitoring records shall be retained in accordance with the Data Retention Policy. Material vendor risks shall be reported through the Enterprise Risk Management framework and reviewed periodically by executive governance committees.
